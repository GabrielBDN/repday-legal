The essentials

  • The Live Coach camera never leaves your phone. Posture analysis runs on the device: its feed is neither sent nor kept for this analysis. Only a video you ask to film is saved on your phone.
  • RepDay does not sell your data, shows no advertising and includes no advertising tracker. The ML Kit pose detection SDK does however send Google technical usage and diagnostic metrics, described below.
  • Your health data is only processed with your explicit consent: weight, height, age, gender, activity level, steps, meals, nutrition targets, reported pain and fatigue. You can refuse or withdraw it at any time: you keep training, without nutrition, steps or the physical profile.
  • A file you import is only sent with your explicit consent, asked for each file, because it may contain health data. You can withdraw it at any time from the import screen.
  • Friends is optional. Until you turn it on, nobody can add you. Once on, your friends only see what you choose to share, and the three shares (workouts, programs, records) start off. There is no public profile and no member search. You can write to your friends: a conversation can only be read by the two of you. You can also post text and photos that only you and your friends see, and like or comment on their posts; your photos are reduced and stripped of their metadata, location included, before being stored.
  • You can delete your account from the app or from the web, without contacting us. Data deletion is detailed below.

2. What data is processed

Account data

Email address, display name, profile photo if you add one (you can remove it at any time), and depending on how you sign in: a password (stored only as a hash, never in clear text) or a Google or Apple account identifier. Signing in with Google or Apple gives us no access to your contacts, emails or storage. With Sign in with Apple, we also keep, encrypted, a token provided by Apple: it is only used to revoke the authorization given to RepDay when you delete your account, as Apple requires.

Health and fitness data

Weight, height, age, gender and activity level, when you enter them; activity (daily step total) and step goal if you use step tracking; meals and nutrition targets (see “Nutrition data”); pain and fatigue you report during a workout of a cyclic program. We treat them as falling under Article 9 of the GDPR (special category), which gives them stronger protection.

This data is only processed with your explicit consent, collected separately from the terms: at sign-up, or the first time an existing account opens the app. A checkbox, unticked at first, states it; a button lets you continue without it. We record each of your decisions: consent, refusal or withdrawal, its date and the version of the text shown.

Without this consent, you use programs, workouts, Live Coach and progression as usual. Nutrition, steps and the physical profile stay off, and reporting pain or fatigue asks for your consent again.

Training data

Workouts, exercises, sets, loads, reps, durations, personal records, estimated maximum strength, progression decisions and deload periods, as well as your experience level, goal, training frequency and equipment. This data is the core of the service.

Nutrition data

Logged meals, foods, favorite foods, water intake and nutrition targets, when you use this feature. If you are linked to a coach, you can choose to show them this log (see section 4).

Imported programs and logs

If you import a file (Excel, CSV, Word or PDF), we process its content, name, size and fingerprint, which is used to spot a file already imported. What you approve becomes a program in your account and, if the file contains them, past performances (date, exercise, load, reps, RPE or RIR, notes) and nutrition profiles. A file can contain anything you wrote in it, including personal notes or health information: remove them before importing if you do not want them processed. Excel and CSV tables that RepDay recognizes are read by our server alone. With a Premium or Coach Premium plan, and if you have allowed your data to be sent to the AI, the text of other files and the names of unknown exercises are sent to our artificial intelligence provider (see section 4). Without a subscription, nothing is sent to it: the analysis stops and the text read is erased at once.

Before each upload, the app shows you this information and asks for your explicit consent for that file: a checkbox, unticked at first. Without it, the file is neither sent nor copied, and you can create your program by hand. We record proof of your consent: the date, the version of the text shown, the file fingerprint and, if applicable, the date of your withdrawal.

Live Coach and camera

Posture analysis uses your phone's camera through a pose detection library that runs entirely on the device. The feed and images used for this analysis are neither recorded nor sent to our servers or to a third party.

This library (Google ML Kit) may contact Google for its configuration, updates and diagnostics. It sends Google technical information about the device and the app, installation identifiers and usage and performance metrics. According to Google, neither the images and videos analyzed nor the analysis results are sent to its servers. Details published by Google. Google also publishes the list of data collected by ML Kit on Android, including device and app information, installation identifiers, metrics, events and error codes.

What is sent to our servers, and only during a workout, are derived technical events: the type of deviation detected, the phase of the movement, the rep number, a confidence score and derived measurements such as range of motion or tempo. These events describe a movement; they cannot be used to rebuild an image.

Set videos

If you ask to film a set with the raised-hand gesture, the button or the voice command when it is on, a silent video is saved in your device's gallery (RepDay album on Android). It is never sent to our servers. The app's temporary file is deleted after saving to the gallery or if cancelled.

Voice command

The “RepDay, record” command is optional and off by default. You can turn it on in “Coach voice”; the microphone permission is then requested. The microphone is only used to recognize the command during Live Coach listening windows. In normal operation, recognition happens on the device: no audio is stored or sent by RepDay. If no compatible on-device recognition engine is available, the voice command is unavailable; the gesture and the button remain usable.

Step tracking

If you turn on sync, RepDay only reads your daily step total from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS). The only write concerns Apple Watch: if you start a workout from the watch and allow it, a strength training workout (type, start and end) is saved to Apple Health; RepDay reads and saves no other data there, neither heart rate nor calories. You can also enter a total manually. While sync is on, the app rereads the totals of the last seven days when you view the Nutrition or steps screen and when you return to the app; it sends them to our servers with their source (Health Connect or Apple Health) and, within a session, only resends a total already sent if it has changed. No total is sent for a day without steps, or when access is denied or removed. A manually entered total is sent with the source “manual entry”; your goal is kept in your profile. The Nutrition screen shows a seven-day history. This data is not shared with third parties and is not used for advertising. You can stop syncing in the app and remove read permission in Health Connect or Apple Health. Steps and the goal are erased with your account.

Messaging

If you are linked to a coach, or if you write to a friend: the content of your messages and the photos and videos you choose to send, with, for a video, its duration and dimensions. A video filmed from a conversation is sent with its sound. These exchanges take place between two human beings: only the other person has access to them, that is their purpose.

Friends (optional)

Friends stays off until you turn it on. When you do, RepDay creates your personal friend code and records your sharing choices and the date of your agreement. You can only be added with this code, which you share yourself: there is no directory, no search by name or email, no contact import and no public profile. You can renew your code at any time: the old one stops working, and your current friends remain.

What other members see:

  • During a friend request, in either direction: your first name (the first word of your display name) and your profile photo. Someone who enters your code sees them as soon as they send the request, before you accept it.
  • Once the request is accepted: your full display name, your profile photo, the date since which you have been friends and, for each category you choose to share:
    • Workouts: your finished workouts (date, duration, program day name, exercises, muscle groups, sets, loads, reps or durations, total volume), the date of your last workout, the number of workouts over 7 and 30 days and the number of consecutive weeks with at least one workout. Your workouts from the last 30 days appear in your friends' activity feed. A workout in progress or abandoned is never shown.
    • Programs: your personal programs (name, description, active program, days, exercises, sets, reps, RIR, tempo, rest times and notes). Programs assigned by a coach are never shown. Your friends can copy a shared program.
    • Records: your best performances (load, reps, estimated maximum strength and date) on your ten most practiced exercises over the last 90 days, calculated from your finished workouts.

The three categories start off: you can share everything in one tap or choose. Each can be changed at any time, with immediate effect: a category turned off is no longer visible to your friends.

Your friends can encourage a shared workout with a “Bravo”. They see the number of Bravos on a workout; only you see who encouraged you. RepDay also records your friendships: requests, acceptances, refusals, cancellations and removals, with their dates.

You can write to a friend: text, photos and videos. A conversation is only accessible to the two of you, and only while you are friends: a removal, a block or leaving Friends closes it immediately for both of you. It is then erased after 30 days, photos and videos included, unless it is the subject of a report (see section 6). If you become friends again before that, you get it back.

Posts. You can post text, up to 5 photos, or both. Before your first post or comment, RepDay shows you the community rules and records the version you accepted and its date.

  • Photos. You choose each photo from your gallery or take it with the camera; the app reduces it before sending. Our servers straighten it, re-encode it in two sizes (at most 1,600 and 480 pixels per side) and remove all its metadata, including GPS location, capture date and device model. The original photo is never kept.
  • Who sees what. A post is visible to you and your friends, and nobody else. Your friends see your display name, profile photo, the text, the photos, the date, the number of likes and the comments.
  • Likes. Everyone sees the number of likes on a post and whether they liked it themselves. The author is told by a notification with the first name of the person who liked it; there is no public list of who liked it.
  • Comments. A comment is visible to the post's author and all their friends, including people who are not your friends: they then only see your first name (the first word of your display name), never your profile photo. A comment is no longer shown if you stop being friends with the post's author. The author of a post can delete the comments under it.
  • What makes a post disappear. If you remove a friend, block them or are blocked, they no longer see your posts or comments, and you no longer see theirs; likes exchanged between you are erased. A suspended account is no longer visible. If you leave Friends, your posts, comments and likes are deleted.
  • Moderation. A post or comment you report disappears immediately from your screen. Content reported by several people is hidden for everyone pending review; its author still sees it, with a notice. The report keeps a copy of the reported text for this review. A filter rejects clearly prohibited text (hate slurs, child sexual exploitation, incitement to suicide); photos undergo no automated analysis.

A program copied by a friend belongs to them: it is not updated with yours and stays in their account, with the custom exercises it contains, even if you then stop sharing your programs, remove this friend or delete your account.

Never shown to your friends: your email, age, gender, weight, height, levels, goals, nutrition, steps, subscription, your exchanges with a coach or with other friends, your links with a coach, AI-generated reviews, Live Coach technique data and your set videos, except those you send yourself in a conversation.

Friend notifications contain the friend's first name and, for a copy, the program name; never a load or an exercise name. A message notification contains the sender's name and the start of the message, or “Photo” or “Video”. A like or comment notification contains its author's first name, never the comment's text.

From a friend's profile or your conversation, you can report them (or a specific message), block them or remove them. A report records the reported account, the reason, the item concerned and your optional comment; it is submitted for review and may lead to the suspension of the account concerned. Blocking a friend ends your friendship: they no longer see anything of your account and can no longer send you a request. Removing a friend deletes the Bravos and likes exchanged between you, without notifying them. Leaving Friends deletes your code, sharing choices, friendships, Bravos and likes given and received, and your posts and comments; your blocks and reports are kept. If a request you had sent was refused less than 30 days before you left, the record of this refusal is also kept until the end of that period (see section 6).

Notifications

For reminders, RepDay records a notification token and the platform type. The Firebase Cloud Messaging SDK also automatically collects the app version, its technical agent and an installation-specific identifier (FID), which does not by itself identify a person or a physical device. The token kept by RepDay is deleted with the account. Details published by Firebase.

Subscription

If purchases are on, your subscription status, access level and billing events (subscription, renewal, expiry, refund). They are off in the closed test version of 29 September 2026. We never receive your bank details: any payment is handled by Apple or Google.

Technical data

IP address (used to limit abuse), account language, technical logs and anonymized error reports used to fix failures. ML Kit also collects device and app information, installation identifiers, API configuration, performance measurements, usage events and error codes for its diagnostics and usage statistics.

7. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To get a copy of your data, “Download my data”, in Profile, Training report, provides free of charge and without a subscription a JSON file of everything RepDay keeps on your account. Photos and videos remain accessible in the app, or on request.

The most direct way to exercise your right to erasure is to delete your account, from the app or from the deletion page: erasure is immediate and requires no steps with us.

In Friends, you can at any time, from the app, turn off a share, remove a friend, renew your code or leave.

For health data, “Withdraw my consent”, in Profile, Health data, immediately erases your weight, height, age, gender, activity level, steps and step goal, meals, water intake, nutrition targets and profiles, favorite and personal foods, as well as the pain and fatigue reported in your workouts and the progression proposals that mention them. It stops sharing with your coach. Your workouts, programs and records stay.

For file imports, “Erase my import data”, on the import screen, withdraws your consent and immediately erases your imports, drafts, imported performances and nutrition profiles, and stops an analysis in progress. This does not affect processing already carried out. Programs already created stay in your account: you delete them from the Programs screen. For a single file still under review, delete its import.

For other rights, write to contact@repday.fr. If our reply does not satisfy you, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, 75007 Paris, cnil.fr.

8. Security

  • All exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted over HTTPS. Unencrypted connections are refused.
  • Passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes (bcrypt for the oldest), never in clear text.
  • The database is encrypted at rest and is not open to the internet. Backups follow the rotation described in section 6.
  • Authentication tokens are kept in the operating system's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore) and can be revoked.
  • Attachments are stored in a private space, only accessible through temporary, short-lived links.
  • Access is limited by rate controls to prevent abuse.

9. Minimum age

RepDay is reserved for adults, aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about minors. If you find that an account was created by a minor, write to us and we will delete it.
