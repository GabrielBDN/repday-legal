Health warning — read before using RepDay

RepDay is not a medical device and provides no diagnosis, treatment or medical advice. Programs, posture corrections, reviews and nutrition recommendations are sports training aids, generated automatically from the data you enter.

  • Ask a health professional before starting a program, especially in the event of a heart problem, injury, pregnancy, chronic condition or return after a long break.
  • Stop immediately in the event of pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty or feeling unwell, and see a health professional.
  • Live Coach corrections rely on an estimate of your posture by the camera. This estimate may be imprecise or wrong, especially in low light, with loose clothing or partial framing. It does not replace a professional's eye.
  • You alone decide the loads you use and the intensity of your workouts.
